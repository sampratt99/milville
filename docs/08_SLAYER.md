# 08 — Slayer (core unchanged; bonus now multiplies OSRS rolls)

Slayer Master gives contracts (wilderness mobs, `slayer:true`); kills earn points; the shop
sells the **Proctor's Helm** line (Crimson/Gilded dye upgrades — dye rows hide once spent),
earmuffs, mirror shield, rock hammer, the Hunter's Signet (chaos-temple teleport). Some mobs
require a Slayer level/item to damage (kurask etc. in the expansion roster).

**`onTaskBonus(r)`** (+10/14/18% by helm tier, only vs the active contract mob in the wild)
now multiplies the **OSRS attack roll** (and max hit) — see 05 §2. The lostStash reclaim
(wilderness death) fee is paid at the Slayer Master.
